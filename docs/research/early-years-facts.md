# Fact list: the early years, and Iridium

Research pass of 26 November 2026. Raw material for cards, mini-games and levels. Nothing here is in the game unless the last column says so.

**Confidence**

- **A**: a reliable source that was read in full for this pass (two or more where listed).
- **B**: one source only, or a detail relayed second-hand (for example, a colleague's account).
- **C**: found only in a third-party summary of Ashlee Vance's book (Bookey) or in an article that says it was written by AI. Do not put a C fact in the game until it is checked against the book or another source.

Source codes are expanded at the end. Everything is paraphrased; no quotations are stored here.
