Carbon to Orbit: an unofficial Rocket Lab fan game

The story of Rocket Lab, one level at a time. From a rocket bike in a Dunedin workshop to the hundredth Electron, and on to Neutron. Carbon to Orbit is a free, open-source browser game with 30 playable levels and 18 kinds of play. It is a fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Rocket Lab.

You walk a world map through thirty years of Rocket Lab history. Every stop is a real moment, and each one plays differently:

  • Platform levels. Build the rocket bike after hours at the appliance factory, haul pad hardware down the coast road to Māhia, carry the Nasdaq listing across Long Beach.
  • Launch control. A real ascent simulation: call the go/no-go poll, fly the pitch program, throttle through Max-Q, command staging and fairing separation, and circularise with the kick stage.
  • Mini-games. Catch a booster with a helicopter, stretch an orbit to the Moon, find the fault on flight 13, bring a capsule down in the outback, run two launch counts in two hemispheres at once.
  • Story cards and people. The founding, the knighthood and the people who were there, each with sources.

Every level ends with what really happened, hides an easter egg, and links its sources. All art and sound are generated in code, apart from Rocket Lab's official mission patches and a few credited photos. It runs in any modern browser, on a computer or a phone, with nothing to install.
