Carbon to Orbit: who played

Anonymous daily counts by country. Days are UTC. A player is a browser's first level start of the day.

Stats token

These numbers are private. Paste the token set as the Worker's STATS_TOKEN secret; this browser will remember it.

Newest day first. The busiest countries of the range have their own column.

By country

Totals for the range. Players are summed over days, so someone who played on three days counts three times. Select a country to filter the page.

By level

How often each level was started, finished and failed, and with how many stars. One player retrying five times is five starts.
